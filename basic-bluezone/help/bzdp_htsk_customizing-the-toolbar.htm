Customizing the toolbar

The BlueZone Plus VBA toolbar can be customized to suit your needs.

You can customize the following features:
  • Text Labels: Text labels located next to the toolbar items can be turned on or off.
  • Adding and Removing Macro Buttons: Macro buttons can be added or removed from the toolbar.
To toggle text labels on or off:
  1. Click Macro → Properties from the BlueZone toolbar.
  2. Either check or clear the Show Button Labels on ToolBar check box.

To add or remove macro buttons:

  1. Click Macro → Properties from the BlueZone toolbar.
  2. Click Customize.
  3. Select the buttons you want to appear on the toolbar by checking or clearing the corresponding items.
    You can also Add, Rename or Delete macro buttons from the toolbar in this window.
