Submitting print queue jobs to the print log

Print Queue jobs that are not “Spooling” or “Printing” can be manually submitted to the Print Log.

  1. Highlight the desired job(s) in the Print Job Queue window.
  2. Click Action → Submit Job(s) to Log from the menu bar or click the Submit Job(s) to Log icon on the toolbar.
Another method for submitting Print Job Queue jobs to the Print Log is to drag and drop the desired job(s) into the Print Log window.
