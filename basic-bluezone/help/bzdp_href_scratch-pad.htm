Scratch pad

The scratch pad is a text window that you can type, paste, and copy text in a BlueZone session. The text can be formatted, cleared, saved as a text file, or printed. If the scratch pad is closed with text still in the window, the text remains in the window when you reopen the scratch pad.

Opening the scratch pad

To open the scratch pad, click View → Scratch Pad.

Docking the scratch pad

The scratch pad can be docked to the top, bottom, left or right of the display window. To dock the scratch pad, you must enable the docking property. Click View → Properties. On the Power Pads tab, select the Enable Docking check box.

One power pad or scratch pad can be docked per edge of the display window. Power pads or the scratch pad cannot dock to another power pad or scratch pad.
