Adding, viewing, and deleting tabs

  • To add a new tab:
    1. In the BlueZone Tab toolbar, click the Add Tab icon.
    2. Select a BlueZone Display, Printer, or FTP profile, and click Open.
  • To add a tab from an open session window:
    1. In the BlueZone Tab toolbar, click the Tab Window icon and drag it over the open session.
    2. Release the mouse button over the session.
  • To delete a tab:
    1. Click the tab that you want to delete.
    2. In the BlueZone Tab toolbar, click the Delete Tab icon.
    3. In the confirmation window, click Yes.
    You can also press Alt+X to delete the active tab.
  • To make a tab a separate session window:
    1. Click the tab.
    2. Click the Untab Window icon.
  • To view the tabbed sessions in tile view, click the Tile View icon in the BlueZone Tab toolbar or press Alt+Z to toggle tile view.
  • To activate a tab, press Alt+ the tab number. (Alt+1, Alt+2, Alt+3, etc.)
