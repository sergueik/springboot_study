Renaming print jobs

Jobs residing in the Print Queue and the Print Log can be given user-defined names that better describe the job. Print jobs are initially given the name of “DocumentX” where X is a unique job number.

  1. Highlight the desired Print Job.
  2. Click Action → Rename Job from the BlueZone menu bar.
Or, you can also:
  1. Highlight the desired Print Job.
  2. Place your cursor over the label area and click.
  3. Type in the new name and press the Enter key.
